I'm William, an Operations Research and Information Engineering student at Cornell. I like taking machine learning out of the notebook and putting it to work on real problems: markets, buildings, small businesses. Startups are where I feel most at home.

That has meant a weather-trading bot running with real money, a hackathon app that turns a phone scan into indoor AR navigation, and an outreach engine that finds local businesses who need a better web presence. The common thread is automation that leaves the screen and makes a measurable difference.

I'm drawn to entrepreneurship and to people who push on what's possible. If you're building something ambitious in AI, automation, or anything at the edge of tech and the real world, I'd love to hear about it.
